# Vendor Procurement Workflow

1. Identify the business purpose.
2. Identify AI-enabled features.
3. Complete the vendor questionnaire.
4. Review data use.
5. Check documentation.
6. Screen the use case.
7. Assign owner.
8. Decide approve / approve with conditions / reject / escalate.
9. Store evidence.
10. Review periodically.
