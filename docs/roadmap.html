Compliance Roadmap

Interactive timeline of the EU AI Act enforcement milestones.

August 2024

Entry into Force

The EU AI Act officially enters into force 20 days after its publication in the Official Journal.

+6 Months (February 2025)

Prohibited Practices Banned

Unacceptable risk systems (like social scoring, manipulative AI, and untargeted facial recognition scraping) must be completely phased out.

+12 Months (August 2025)

General Purpose AI (GPAI) Rules Apply

Providers of GPAI models must comply with transparency, copyright, and systemic risk assessment obligations.

+24 Months (August 2026)

High-Risk Systems (Annex III) Rules Apply

Full compliance required for high-risk AI systems listed in Annex III (biometrics, critical infrastructure, HR, law enforcement). Fines become fully applicable.

+36 Months (August 2027)

High-Risk Systems (Annex II) Rules Apply

Obligations apply to high-risk AI systems that are already regulated under existing EU harmonization legislation (e.g., medical devices, machinery, toys).
